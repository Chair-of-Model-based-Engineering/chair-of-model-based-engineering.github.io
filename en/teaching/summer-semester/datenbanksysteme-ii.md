# Database Systems II

**Summer semester**

**Event numbers**: 43MBE1011V (Lecture) und 43MBE1012V (Exercise)

**Prerequisites:** Module “Database Systems I” is recommended.

**Exam:** written

## Topics

- Graph databases
  - Basics
  - RDF and LPG
  - Neo4J/Cypher, SPARQL, …

## Procedure

To participate, enroll in the course in Unisono and Moodle.

Lecture and exercise materials as well as announcements are published **[in the Moodle course](https://moodle.uni-siegen.de/course/view.php?id=28753)**.
