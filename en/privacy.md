# Privacy policy

This policy supplements the [privacy policy of the University of Siegen](https://www.uni-siegen.de/datenschutzerklaerung) (in German) with the details specific to this website of the Chair of Model-based Engineering.

## Hosting

This website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
When you open a page, GitHub processes technical access data such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser type in order to deliver the pages and secure the service.
We ourselves have no access to this data.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with § 3 of the North Rhine-Westphalia Data Protection Act (DSG NRW).
The data is transferred to the USA; GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).
GitHub decides how long the data is stored; see the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Cookies and local storage

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking services, and loads no content from third parties.

Your browser's local storage keeps the language of the page you last opened and, if you use the toggle, the light or dark design.
These values stay in your browser and are not transmitted; no consent is required for this (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## Information about members of the chair

Photos, CVs and further personal details of the members of the chair are published only with their consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR); the [privacy policy of the University of Siegen](https://www.uni-siegen.de/datenschutzerklaerung) gives the details.
You may also lodge a complaint with the [State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia](https://www.ldi.nrw.de/) (Art. 77 GDPR).
