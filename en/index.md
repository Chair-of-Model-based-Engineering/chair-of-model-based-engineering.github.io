## Teaching

The **teaching** at our Chair encompasses lectures, seminars and internships for the core subjects **Software Engineering** and **Database Systems** and is aimed at students of computer science of bachelor and master programs and related courses.

## Research

The **research focuses** at our Chair include the following fields of work:

- Model-based design and quality assurance of complex software-systems
- Model-based software engineering and tools
- Software testing and program analysis
- Software product lines and evolution of software
- Semantics of modelling languages
- Formal methods and concurrency theory

[![Research focus: model-based engineering and construction of complex software systems on one side, model-based analysis and quality assurance on the other, connected by tool support](/assets/img/MBE-Research-EN.png){: .center style="width: 70%"}](/en/research/)

Particular focus is on the following problem domains:

- Embedded and Cyber-Physical Software-Systeme
- Safety- and Mission-critical Software-Systeme
